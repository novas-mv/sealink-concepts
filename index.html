02

Where we are

  1. Sealink leads the brand. Meatplus stays the name of the retail outlets.
  2. The logo stays as it is. We build a graphic language from its two parts: the red A and the navy sweep.
  3. The website is both a corporate site and a product catalogue, with room to add trade login and online ordering later.
  4. These are concepts to choose from, not final designs.

Items in square brackets, such as [PHOTO] or [HOURS], mark content we still need from Sealink. They are placeholders and will be filled in with your material. Photos are sample stock images; we will replace them with photos of Sealink's own cold store, team and products.

08

What we need from Sealink

These items replace the placeholders in the designs. Nothing here blocks the choice of concept.

  • Photos of the cold store, the team, the trucks and the outlets.
  • Product photos on white. Partners can often supply these.
  • Product list as one spreadsheet: name, brand, category, pack size, storage band, SKU code.
  • Storage temperatures for frozen, chilled and fresh, and the cold store capacity.
  • Certificates: halal (body, number, expiry) and MFDA or other food safety licences.
  • Original logo files as vectors (AI, PDF or SVG), for both Sealink and Meatplus.
  • Partner approval to show the Emborg, Sumich and IOEC logos and the words "sole distributor".
  • Outlet details: address, phone and opening hours for each Meatplus outlet.
