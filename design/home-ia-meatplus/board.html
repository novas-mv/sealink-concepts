Home page order and a real place for Meatplus

Look: Navy and Paper. Current pages captured at 1440 px and 375 px. Options are low-fidelity wireframes. They show order and weight only, not final styling. Orange marks a problem, or a block that changed.

1. Home page

  1. 1Meatplus is one thin strip at the very bottom, under the trade band. Small logo, one line. It reads like a footnote, not a well-known shop.
  2. 2In the first screen, a home shopper sees nothing that tells them they can buy from Meatplus. The only path is at the bottom or in the footer.
  3. 3"Why kitchens buy from Sealink" comes before categories. On a phone it is about 930 px of rows. A buyer who wants to browse must scroll past it.
  4. 4The main trade action (send your list, get prices) comes late: about 2,200 px down on a phone. Trade and Meatplus are mixed together at the end.

Look at
  • 1: In both options, Meatplus gets a full section with its logo, a short "who we are", and outlet cards. It links to the Outlets page.
  • 2: A puts one quiet line in the hero. B puts two equal cards right under the hero. B gives Meatplus more weight early, but it splits the first screen between two audiences.
  • 3: Categories move above "Why" in both. Buyers can browse sooner.
  • 4: A keeps the trade band as the last trade block, then hands over to Meatplus. B moves it higher, so the trade action comes sooner on a phone.
  • Why I pick A: trade buyers stay the main audience. Home shoppers still find Meatplus in the first screen and at the end.
